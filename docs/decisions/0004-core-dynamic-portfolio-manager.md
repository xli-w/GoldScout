# Decision: Stable core and bounded dynamic direction

Status: accepted direction; role accounting/local awareness implemented,
policy/research/execution gated

Dates: 2026-11-16; investor clarification 2026-11-17

The [current mandate](../investment-research-context.md) includes selected savings,
first-home LISA and longer-term investments, not household budgeting. The
illustrative 80-90% core / 10-20% dynamic range approves no targets or risk
budget. The dynamic monitoring denominator is non-LISA investment holdings,
excluding savings, LISA and all cash.

## Decision

- Keep asset class and management role independent. Exact core/dynamic/
  unassigned attribution conserves balances and isolates debits; account
  transfers preserve roles and role-to-role movements remain disabled.
  Attribution is not custody verification or risk policy.
- Favor diversified low-turnover unleveraged core exposure. Dynamic research
  must be finite, interpretable and compared with matched all-core funding.
  Do not count core gains as alpha or automatically refill dynamic losses.
  Leverage, shorts, crypto and speculative options are not newly approved.
- Prioritize source-linked measurement, monitoring, calendar/AI awareness and
  human review. Intelligence is advisory and private evidence stays local.
  No AI-driven policy rewrite, risk expansion or experiment promotion.
- Proposals remain blocked. Human approval of future individual actions comes
  before any separately implemented revocable standing authority.

## Decisions required before any approved policy

Bind exact designated accounts/wrappers/products/currencies; core/dynamic and
asset-class targets; unassigned handling; explicit capital/position/aggregate/
concentration/turnover/cost/loss budgets; replenishment/retirement and profit-
transfer rules; freshness/reconciliation/review/expiry/failure rules; and
authenticated investor approval of the immutable policy version and digest.
Unknown values have no defaults. Caps never guarantee realized-loss bounds.

Funding recommendations are not implementation: prohibit automatic core-funded
loss refill; separately approve any explicitly assigned external dynamic
contribution or human-confirmed dynamic-to-core profit transfer. A toggle may
disable or choose already authorized behavior, never broaden authority.

Any future decision must bind approved policy and unchanged journal revision,
causal evidence/coverage and assumptions. Incomplete evidence blocks the
affected decision; observed money is not settled buying power and proposed
sales do not fund purchases.

Standing execution would require independently designed venue-specific durable
intent, ambiguous-submit recovery, fill ownership, settlement, partial fills,
restart/revocation and approval evidence. None is implemented. No synthetic
trace, backtest, public API documentation or browser setting supplies authority.

Use the [research protocol](../backtesting-protocol.md) before comparisons and
[retirement assessment](0006-portfolio-only-retirement.md) for reuse decisions.
Preserve all previous trial/exposure/holdout history.
