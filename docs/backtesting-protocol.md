# Portfolio research protocol

Read the [investor mandate and preserved search history](investment-research-context.md)
before research. This protocol permits no dataset acquisition, comparative
return run, broker call or operational-store access. A valid outcome is fail or
inconclusive; never force a winner.

## Stop gates before product-performance work

1. Establish exact product/share-class/ISIN, UK retail/account/wrapper eligibility,
   currency, charges, increments and source/date of each fact. Ticker support,
   an issuer page or an API schema is not account permission.
2. Verify public/operator-supplied persistent data rights and historical
   vintages. Freeze raw inputs, license/provider/retrieval evidence, timestamp
   semantics, currency, price basis/actions and hashes. No paid/authenticated
   broker feeds, scraping without rights, synthetic gap filling or unapproved
   pre-inception proxies. Short/missing regimes mean limited or inconclusive.
3. Verify eligible actual cash terms, effective/publication clocks, accrual,
   day-count, compounding, fees and tax. BoE quoted-rate proxies and NatWest
   historical 0% are explicitly labelled counterfactuals, not actual earned yield.
4. Validate total-return/actions, distribution entitlement/ex/pay clocks and
   point-in-time FX direction, publication, executable conversion and costs.
   Avoid double-counting accumulating income or embedded product expenses.
5. Establish one funded chronological GBP portfolio clock with causal marks,
   simultaneous-event ordering, cash/quantity conservation, internal transfers,
   external-flow-neutral performance and explicit execution/cost conventions.
   Final-cutoff views cannot supply future knowledge to earlier decisions.
6. Approve actual core/dynamic scope, weights, capital/risk/cost/loss budgets,
   replenishment/profit-transfer and failure rules, then freeze the finite
   comparison, candidates, dates, metrics, costs and admission rules before
   viewing returns. An illustrative allocation supplies no default.

If any gate is unmet, stop before loading histories or producing comparisons.
The 15-17 November public screens did not close them. No licensed retained
product histories, complete vintages, verified product/account access or frozen
policy comparison were established. Monitoring and an opening journal do not
close these gaps.

## Predeclaration, selection and confirmation

- Preserve the entire earlier trial/exposure/holdout history described in the
  [mandate](investment-research-context.md#historical-research-constraints-remain-binding).
  Retiring machinery does not reset counts, reserved samples or consumed holdouts.
  Exact registry history has not been inspected by this retirement.
- Keep **60 distinct configurations total**, including baselines and
  manual/failed/abandoned variants. It is a ceiling, not a fresh allocation.
  Do not escape prior exposure by changing directories, registry or dataset IDs.
- Freeze products, hypotheses, parameters, signal/feature/availability rules,
  calendar, cost/tax/contribution scenarios, selection windows, natural versus
  forced exits, seeds, trial count and decision rules; hash the specification.
- For dynamic strategies compare sleeve and combined net GBP outcomes with a
  matched all-core counterfactual using identical funding, costs, tax/wrapper and
  event clocks. Report beta, overlapping exposure, FX, income, costs and active
  contribution. Internal sleeve transfers and core gains are not active alpha.
- Run fixed base and predeclared 1.5x/2.0x variable-cost cases during selection,
  keeping fixed expenses explicit. Include spreads/slippage, partial/missed
  fills, gaps, taxes where evidenced and operational complexity. Stop prices
  and planned caps are not guaranteed losses.
- Predeclare adequate independent observations and dependence/trial-aware
  uncertainty. Assess drawdown depth/duration, tails, concentration, turnover,
  natural exits and regime dependence, not only CAGR/Sharpe/win rate.
- Lock at most the predeclared candidate before **one genuinely unexposed,
  single-use report-only confirmation**. No passing candidate means no holdout
  opening, including a baseline-only fallback. A failed/inconclusive holdout
  cannot choose a replacement or be tuned against.
- A new idea requires a registered research cycle and prior-search accounting,
  not relabelling exposed periods. Selection, prospective shadow evidence,
  independently reviewed venue engineering and any separately approved account
  trial are distinct gates; no return result grants execution authority.

The old GBP 1,000/+5-point NatWest hurdle, stop-risk/leverage/crypto profiles and
20% drawdown review flag remain historical provenance, not current passive
allocation rules. Neither the old 30%-in-six-month aspiration nor a positive
point estimate is an admission rule or forecast.

Existing news/LLM trading exclusions remain. A future calendar/AI feature needs
separate prospective signal/failure-policy approval, dated receipt/validated
completion, model/prompt/input hashes and every manual/model trial. Private
account evidence never goes to hosted AI. Today's model on historical text
cannot establish causal out-of-sample evidence.

## Retained engineering and its limits

[`research_data`](campaign-dataset.md) is a local-only, content-addressed importer
with source manifests/checksums and strict reopen validation. Scoped XLON daily
readiness checks declared GBP/pence units, sessions, availability and cash-rate
age. Normalized and FX views require complete selected observations at supplied
cutoffs or reject. A cutoff pass proves neither source truth/vintages nor
complete causal transaction coverage, executable FX, entitlement or returns.
The pure `account_fills` helper checks ordered synthetic long-only GBP cash,
fees and quantities only, not valuation, accrual, risk or performance.

The [synthetic portfolio simulator](investment.md#synthetic-simulator-mechanics-engineering-only)
checks genuinely reusable causal GBP portfolio mechanics: next-open allocation,
cash constraints, exact increments, external-flow unitization, split handling,
distribution receivables/payment and explicit rate timing. It accepts synthetic
fixtures only, verifies no source provenance and has no importer/CLI/store
integration. It omits the verified product, tax, FX and execution model needed
for research. Engineering test success is not a feasibility pass or return result.

## Required evidence for any future approved run

Retain canonical preregistration/hash and complete trial ledger; eligible
product/account/terms and licensed source/vintage evidence; fixed benchmarks,
costs/tax/contribution scenarios; chronological GBP cash/equity/action/FX and
cost audit trails; failed/invalid results; software/dependency fingerprints;
uncertainty and regime/turnover/drawdown/cost attribution; selection versus
locked-confirmation outcomes; and independent read-only review. Explicitly
distinguish executed evidence, assumptions, unsupported risks and conclusions
not justified. Never claim profitability, suitability or live readiness.
